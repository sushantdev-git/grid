Architecture & Design

Engineered for absolute sovereignty.

Every layer of Grid is built from the silicon up to protect communication when institutions, infrastructure, and cellular networks fail.

Hyper-Local BLE Mesh & Multi-Hop Flooding

Communicate completely off the internet. Grid forms an ad-hoc, self-healing mesh topology directly over Bluetooth Low Energy (2.4 GHz). Packets hop across intermediate nodes with degree-based TTL clamping (7 ➔ 5), 1000-entry LRU deduplication, randomized relay jitter (10–220ms), and split-horizon filtering.

BitChat v2.0 Wire Spec Sub-100ms Latency Zero Cellular Required

Off-Grid PTT Voice Notes

High-utility walkie-talkie audio over low-bandwidth mesh. Voice is compressed using low-bitrate Opus (~1.5 KB per 5s snippet), sliced into sliding-window wire fragments, and delivered across BLE with real-time waveform playback.

Zero Accounts, Keys Only

No phone numbers, no emails, no logins, no metadata harvesting. Your dual Curve25519 (X25519) and Ed25519 cryptographic keypairs generated entirely on-device are your sole identity. Verify peers via 60-digit safety numbers.

Noise_XX Protocol E2EE

Mutual authentication with cryptographic forward secrecy. Built using the formal Noise_XX_25519_ChaChaPoly_SHA256 handshake pattern with 1024-bit sliding-window replay protection. Intermediate relay nodes cannot inspect or tamper with message contents.

Dual-Transport Nostr Bridge

Local BLE mesh seamlessly bridges to the global decentralized Nostr relay network whenever an internet connection is available. Reach mutual contacts across continents through censorship-resistant WebSocket relays without leaking metadata.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Grid really work without any cellular service or internet? +
Yes. Grid utilizes Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) direct peer-to-peer connections. Even during severe power outages, natural disasters, or cellular ISP shutdowns, any two or more Grid devices within radio proximity can chat, exchange voice notes, and relay messages multi-hop to other nearby nodes.
How far can messages travel over the BLE mesh? +
A single direct Bluetooth hop typically ranges from 10 to 50 meters depending on obstacles and device antennas. However, because Grid uses controlled multi-hop flooding with a Time-To-Live (TTL) of up to 5 hops, a message can effortlessly hop through intermediate phones and laptops across several hundred meters or throughout an entire building without internet.
Can intermediate relay nodes read my messages or listen to my voice notes? +
Never. Private conversations and voice notes are cryptographically secured end-to-end using the Noise Protocol Framework (Noise_XX with Curve25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD, and SHA-256). Intermediate relay nodes only see the routing header (TTL, packet nonce, and dummy-padded byte buckets). They possess zero ability to decrypt or tamper with the payload.
What is the role of Nostr in Grid? +
Nostr acts as an optional global bridge. When your phone has an active internet or Wi-Fi connection, Grid can communicate with remote contacts across the world via decentralized Nostr relays, using your same cryptographic identity keypair. When internet drops, Grid seamlessly continues operating over local BLE mesh without missing a beat.
What happens when I trigger Panic Wipe? +
Panic Wipe is designed for physical duress and emergencies. It instantly overwrites your private keys in memory with zeros, flushes all volatile session states, and executes a destructive SQLite database wipe. There is zero confirmation dialog or delay, guaranteeing immediate forensic eradication.

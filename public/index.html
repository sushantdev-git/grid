CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Sovereign Resilience

Grid combines low-level Bluetooth radio hardware with modern cryptography to guarantee communication when conventional networks fail.

Autonomous Multi-Hop BLE Mesh

No central routers or cell towers. Messages hop node-to-node across 2.4 GHz Bluetooth LE. Controlled flooding with degree-adaptive TTL clamping ($7 \to 5$) and 1,000-entry LRU deduplication prevents broadcast storms.

Range: 100m+ per hop Relay Jitter: 10–220ms

Off-Grid PTT Voice Notes

Push-to-talk voice messaging compressed with low-bitrate Opus speech mode. Packets are sliced into 469-byte MTU frames and reassembled with out-of-order jitter buffering.

Noise_XX Protocol E2EE

Mutual identity authentication and ephemeral Diffie-Hellman key exchange. Guarantees Forward Secrecy (FS) and Post-Compromise Security (PCS) without central key servers.

Dual-Transport Nostr Bridge

When internet connectivity is available, Grid automatically bridges across decentralized Nostr relays (NIP-01) to route messages globally without compromising on metadata privacy.

Instant Physical Panic Zeroization

Under physical threat or device seizure, Grid executes an instant zeroization sweep. Private keys are overwritten with pseudo-random noise before being purged. Zero confirmation dialogs, zero time delay.

Wipe Latency: < 50ms Zero Dialog Delay
